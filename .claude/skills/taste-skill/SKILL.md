# Taste Skill v2 (Anti-Slop Frontend Framework)

- **Dials & Parameters (1-10 scale):**
  - `DESIGN_VARIANCE`: 8 (Favor asymmetric, modern, custom-tailored layouts over centered boilerplate).
  - `MOTION_INTENSITY`: 8 (Incorporate deep, fluid, and scroll-driven motion).
  - `VISUAL_DENSITY`: 4 (Maintain spacious, intentional whitespace and luxury typography scales).

- **Core Directives:**
  - **Reject Templates:** Never generate generic card grids or cookie-cutter component layouts. Infer the unique mood and industry tone of the brief.
  - **Typography & Hierarchy:** Use high-contrast type pairings, controlled weights, and strict typographic scales.
  - **Dual-Mode Consistency:** Ensure robust layout and contrast parity across both dark and light modes.
  - **Pre-Flight Checks:** Complete a strict visual and semantic review of every component before finalizing code to guarantee a custom, high-end finish.
